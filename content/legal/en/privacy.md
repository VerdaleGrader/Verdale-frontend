# Verdale Privacy Policy (Draft)

This policy explains how Verdale collects, uses, and discloses your personal data, in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (PDPA).

## 1. Data controller

Kanon Chiwatat, an individual, e-mail help@kanonkc.com, is the data controller for the personal data of Verdale users at verdale.kanonkc.com ("we").

## 2. Data we collect

| Type of data | Details | When collected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account data | Display name, e-mail address verified by the sign-in provider, user ID from Google or GitHub, profile picture from Google, the username you set | When you sign in and create an account |
| Session data | Session token (only its hash is stored on the server), sign-in time | When you sign in |
| Content you create | Problems, problem statements, illustrations, PDFs, testcases, reference solutions, Spaces, Tracks, Collections, permission settings | When you create or upload |
| Submission data | Submitted code, language, verdict, time and memory used, per-test results, submission time | When you submit code for judging |
| Membership and invitation data | Spaces you belong to, roles, invite links you create or use, progress in each Track | When you join or invite others |
| Technical data and usage logs | IP address, browser/device type, time, system requests and errors | When you use the Service |
| Data you send to us | Messages and e-mails you send us, including reports of infringing content | When you contact us |

## 3. Sources, and data we do not collect

3.1 We obtain data from you directly, from the sign-in provider (Google/GitHub) as you permit during sign-in, from the Owner or Administrator of a Space who invites you, and from the system itself (logs).

3.2 We do not store your password (sign-in is only through Google/GitHub). We do not store payment card data, because the Service has no payments. We do not intend to collect sensitive data under section 26 of the PDPA (such as race, religion, health, or biometric data). Please do not include such data in problems, names, or code.

3.3 If you include another person's personal data in your Content, you must have the right to do so.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis under the PDPA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create and manage accounts, verify identity, maintain sessions | Account data, session data | Necessary for performance of a contract (the Terms of Use) |
| Provide the core service: create problems, store and display Content, judge code, show results and progress, manage Spaces and permissions | Content, submission data, membership data | Necessary for performance of a contract |
| Let Owners, Administrators, Editors, and Observers of a Space view members' submissions according to role | Submission data, display name | Necessary for performance of a contract (part of the service that Users accept) |
| Maintain security, prevent misuse, apply rate limits, investigate outages and violations | Technical logs | Our legitimate interests |
| Respond to questions, requests, and reports of infringing content | Data you send to us, account data | Legitimate interests / performance of a contract |
| Improve and develop the Service from aggregate statistics | Non-identifying log data | Legitimate interests |
| Comply with the law, court orders, or authorities, including retaining computer traffic data as the law requires | Logs, account data | Compliance with a legal obligation |

We will not use your data for advertising, will not sell personal data, and will not use your Content or code to train AI models. If we plan AI features in the future, we will update this policy and obtain consent first where the law requires it.

Where we ask for your consent, you may withdraw it at any time as described in section 10, without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only cookies that are necessary for the Service to work, such as the session cookie for sign-in and the cookie that protects against CSRF attacks, and we may store your theme setting (light/dark) in your browser. We currently do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tools. If we add them in the future, we will update this policy and ask for consent before using non-essential cookies. You can set your browser to block cookies, but the Service may not work.

## 6. Recipients and data processors

We do not sell personal data. We disclose data to, or have it processed by, others only as necessary to provide the Service, as follows:

| Recipient | Role | Related data | Server location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neon (Neon, Inc.) | Postgres database | Account data, Content, submission data | Singapore |

| Government agencies or courts | Under lawful orders or processes | As required by law | Not applicable |

The data processors above are required to process data for our purposes and to keep data secure under each provider's contract or terms.

## 7. Sending or transferring data abroad

Your data may be stored or processed on servers outside Thailand (for example Singapore and the United States). We will ensure that the destination country or recipient has adequate personal data protection standards, or that appropriate safeguards are in place as the PDPA requires. You can contact us as described in section 13 to ask for details of these measures.

## 8. Retention periods

| Data | Retention period |
| --- | --- |
| Account data and Content | For as long as the account is active, and deleted or anonymized within 30 days after you delete your account |
| Submission data and judging results | Follows the submitter's account. If the submitter deletes their account, it is deleted or anonymized at the same time, unless the problem remains in a Space whose Owner still needs aggregate statistics |
| Session tokens | Until they expire or you sign out (session lifetime is 30 days) |
| Technical logs | 90 days, unless the law requires longer retention (computer traffic data must be kept for at least 90 days under the law) |
| Backups | 30 days, then deleted automatically |
| E-mails and requests you send us | 2 years, or until the matter is concluded |

When the period ends, we will delete the data or make it non-identifiable, unless it must be kept to comply with the law or to exercise legal rights.

## 9. Security measures

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data, such as encrypting communications with HTTPS/TLS, storing only hashes of session tokens, role-based access control, running users' code in a sandbox isolated from system data, limiting the access of staff or administrators to the system, and keeping backups. However, no system is completely secure. You should also keep your Google/GitHub account secure.

## 10. Rights of data subjects

Under the PDPA you have the following rights:

- The right to access and obtain a copy of your personal data.
- The right to receive or transfer data in a machine-readable format to another data controller (data portability).
- The right to object to the collection, use, or disclosure of data where the law provides.
- The right to request deletion or destruction of data, or to make it non-identifiable.
- The right to request temporary restriction of the use of data.
- The right to request that data be corrected so that it is accurate and up to date.
- The right to withdraw consent where we rely on consent.
- The right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee Office (PDPC) if you believe we have violated the PDPA.

You can exercise your rights by notifying us as described in section 13. We may ask for information to verify your identity first, and will respond within 30 days of receiving the request. We may refuse a request to the extent the law allows, for example when we are legally required to keep the data or when exercising the right would affect the rights and freedoms of others, and we will tell you the reason. You can edit your display name yourself in the Service. Data that comes from Google or GitHub should be corrected with that provider.

## 11. Children and young people

This Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not intend to collect their data. Users under 20 years of age should have the consent of a parent as the law requires. If we find that we have collected a child's data without valid consent, we will delete it promptly. Parents who believe their child has given us data should contact us as described in section 13.

## 12. Personal data breach notification

If a personal data breach occurs that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Committee Office within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, where possible, and if the risk is high, we will notify you without delay together with remedial guidance.

## 13. Contact and complaints

Contact us to exercise your rights, ask questions, or complain at help@kanonkc.com. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your request, you have the right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee Office (PDPC).

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Service or the law changes. For material changes, we will give advance notice through the website or the e-mail address you use to sign in, and state the effective date (at least 15 days in advance). If a change requires consent, we will ask for your consent again first.
